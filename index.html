Dashboard

Welcome to your MI workspace. Choose an option from the left menu. The selected section opens here without moving the page up or down.

Retrieve shared information

Sign in to your own MI account, then enter a creator's Share Code. The shared project will be saved in your Received Projects. The original owner keeps ownership.

Shared information will appear here.

Your account

Create an account or sign in to save and manage your information. You can create a separate student share code for content you choose to share.

Connect to your account to begin.

Save information

Use exam-style numbering: 1, 1(a), 1(b), 2, 2(a). This outline is saved with the project and shared with its Share Code.

If you set a password, use at least 8 characters. Share it separately from the Share Code.

Up to 20 files per project; maximum 25 MB per file. Files are private until you create/share a project code.

Your generated Retrieve Code will appear here.

Manage my shared projects

Choose one of your existing Share Codes to set, change, or remove its project password, or temporarily turn sharing off. This area is available only after you sign in.

Load your Share Codes to manage them.

Received Projects

Projects you have accepted from other MI users appear here. The original creator keeps ownership. Projects disappear from this list if their creator turns sharing off.

Your received projects will appear here.

Send Feedback

Share your suggestions, report a problem, or tell us how MI-RETRIEVE-CODE can be improved.

Your feedback will be submitted to MI-RETRIEVE-CODE.
Sharing and privacy: Your saved information remains private unless you create a student share code for it. Anyone who has that share code can view the shared information, so only share codes for content you intend to distribute. Never put a Supabase secret key in this website.
